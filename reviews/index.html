What keeps coming up

Patience. It is in more reviews than any other word. One student completed her lessons in the ninth month of pregnancy and credits the calm approach for making it possible. Another had been through several instructors before finding one who explained things properly.

First attempt passes. Several reviewers mention passing on the first try, including one who passed CBT&A with an auditor sitting in the car. Another completed the full licence test after just two training sessions.

Clear explanation. Breaking a task down and making sure it is understood before it is attempted on the road. It sounds obvious. It is apparently not universal.

If you have had lessons with Gopi, a review helps other learners more than you would think. It is the main way people find a driving instructor now.
